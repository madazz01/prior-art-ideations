# Vehicle Service Intelligence Platform

**Author:** Bryan Horsfield  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** Prior art disclosure — public timestamp  

## Concept

A platform where vehicle owners register their car details (make, model, year, odometer, service history) and the system:

1. **Tracks service intervals** by kilometres driven — automatically knows when the next oil change, tyre rotation, brake inspection, or major service is due based on manufacturer specifications
2. **Proactive reminders** — notifies the owner before the service window, not after it's overdue
3. **Automated quote aggregation** — reaches out to 3+ local service centres on the owner's behalf, returns quotes for comparison, owner accepts or declines without making a single phone call
4. **Service history log** — maintains a complete digital record of all work done, parts used, costs paid — transferable when the car is sold

## Key Differentiator

The quote aggregation removes the primary friction point in car ownership: the effort of finding and comparing local mechanics. The platform does the legwork. The owner sees options, picks one, confirms.

## Business Model

- Free tier: reminders only
- Paid tier: automated quote requests, service history export, resale report
- B2B: service centres pay for qualified lead delivery (reverse marketplace model)
