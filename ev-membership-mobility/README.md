# EV Membership Mobility — The Car Card Model

**Author:** Bryan Horsfield  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** Prior art disclosure — public timestamp  
**Note:** Author states this concept has been held for several years prior to this writing.

## Concept

A post-ownership mobility model where consumers do not purchase vehicles — they purchase a membership card that grants access to a fleet of electric vehicles.

## Core Mechanics

- **Monthly membership fee** (e.g. $100/month) replaces car ownership, insurance, registration, and maintenance costs
- **All-electric fleet** — no combustion vehicles
- **Zero downtime guarantee** — when a vehicle requires service or repair, the member swaps to another vehicle immediately. No waiting. No loan cars. No inconvenience.
- **Annual upgrade option** — members can upgrade to a newer model annually for a fee, or remain on current tier
- **Accessory/personalisation layer** — members can customise their assigned vehicle (seat profiles, connectivity preferences, exterior options) for an additional fee

## Key Innovation

The swap-and-go repair model eliminates the single largest pain point of car ownership: downtime during repairs. Under traditional ownership, a broken car means no car. Under this model, a broken car means walking to the nearest swap point and driving away in minutes.

This fundamentally changes the economics of EV adoption — range anxiety and repair anxiety (the two primary objections) are both dissolved by the membership model.

## Distinction from Existing Models

- **Car sharing (Zipcar, GoGet):** Per-trip, not membership. No personalisation. No upgrade path.
- **Leasing:** Fixed term, fixed vehicle, full downtime risk, insurance required separately.
- **Canoo/similar subscription attempts:** Did not solve the swap-and-go zero-downtime problem at scale. No accessory/personalisation layer.
- **This model:** Permanent membership, zero downtime, upgrade optionality, personalisation — closer to a phone plan than a car lease.

## Business Model

- Monthly membership tiers (base / mid / premium) by vehicle category
- Annual upgrade fee
- Accessory/personalisation subscriptions
- Fleet operated and maintained by the platform — members never interact with maintenance
- B2B fleet extension: corporate memberships for business travel

## Prior Art Note

The specific combination of membership card model + zero-downtime swap + personalisation layer + annual upgrade constitutes the novel claim.
